Install StreamBox on your TV

Put StreamBox on your Android TV or Fire TV, sign in once with a code, and it stays connected.

⬇ Download for Android TV Open in browser instead

Install on Android TV or Fire TV

  1. On the TV, open Settings → Device Preferences → Security and turn on Unknown sources.
  2. Install a sideload app such as Downloader from the Play Store or Amazon Appstore.
  3. In Downloader, type the address of this page and press Go.
  4. Choose Download for Android TV above, then Install.
  5. Open StreamBox from your apps row. A code appears on screen.

Pair it

  1. On your phone or computer, go to your account page and open Activate device.
  2. Type the code showing on the TV.
  3. The TV says paired within a few seconds. It stays paired after a restart.
No Android TV to hand? Choose Open in browser instead. It is the same app and pairs exactly the same way — good enough for a demo, and it works on any smart TV with a browser.
